The squad

Five heroes, all seventeen

You control one at a time. The other four recover health on the bench, so swapping is both a tactic and a heal. Dealing damage fills your Surge bar and unlocks a temporary greater form — or hit FORCE to trigger it on demand.

Tier 6

Then there's Deathbringer

DEATHBRINGER

A pitch-black ent the size of a house, sheeted in living mucus that drips off him constantly, with two burning eyes set in a hollow face. He manipulates darkness and kills by touch. He is the arch nemesis of all five.

Deliberately not a health sponge — a challenge, not a slog. He hits far harder than anything else in the city, and dies sooner.

  • Death Touch — heavy damage plus Wither, which cuts all your healing to 30%
  • Grasp of the Grove — tendrils erupt in a ring and root you in place
  • Root Surge — black spears burst from the ground beneath you
  • Darkness — he blinds the arena and shadow orbs hunt you through it
  • At half health the grove wakes: faster, harder, permanently dimmed

Two ways to play

An open city, or a side-on duel

CAMPAIGN

Top-down, 6400×4800 of procedural city. Threat rises the further you get from the central plaza — level 1 in the middle, level 4 at the edges. Six marked arenas hold a level-5 Colossus, and the grove on the east edge holds Deathbringer. Every tier has its own trick: swarms, ranged bolts, front-facing shields you have to flank, blink-stalkers that dodge.

VERSUS

Side-on, best of three, framed like a fighting game — so you can actually see the character designs and what their powers look like. Pick your fighter, pick your opponent (Deathbringer by default, or any teammate), pick a difficulty. These fighters are real 3D models, lit and casting shadows on the arena floor. Same kits, same damage, same forms as the campaign.
