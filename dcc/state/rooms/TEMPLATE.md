## Compaction

Every 10 room cards, write `F<floor>-DIGEST-<n>.md` collapsing those ten into **five
bullets total** using the same headings, then the individual cards are archive-only — never
re-read them unless the player returns to that specific room.

Digests compact the same way at the floor boundary: one floor = one five-bullet card.
