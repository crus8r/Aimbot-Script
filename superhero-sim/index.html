VANGUARD

Five heroes. One fractured city. Swap freely, break your Surge, hold the line.

CONTROLS

Touch

Left half of the screen — drag anywhere to move.

Right cluster — Attack, two abilities, Dash.

Drag off an action button to aim it manually; otherwise it auto-targets the nearest enemy.

Tap a portrait at the top-left to swap hero instantly.

FORCE fills your Surge and triggers the greater form immediately — this is a test build.

Keyboard

WASD / Arrows — move

J or Space — attack  ·  K — ability 1  ·  L — ability 2

Shift — dash  ·  Q / E — Surge Form  ·  F — force form

1–5 or Tab — swap hero  ·  C — extra action  ·  Esc — pause

THE CITY

Threat rises the further you travel from the central plaza — level 1 near the middle, level 4 at the edges. Six marked arenas hold a level 5 Colossus. Enemy tiers each have their own trick: swarms, ranged bolts, front-facing shields that must be flanked, blink-stalkers that dodge — and the boss.

VERSUS

Side-on, best of three. Stick up to jump, stick down to guard.
